About

I recently graduated with a BSc in Computer Science from HiLCoE School of Computer Science in Addis Abeba, Ethiopia (2022–2026). I build web and mobile applications, and I care most about whether they work correctly before users see them.

Testing is a large part of my work. My recent projects, including ShopBot, have automated test suites, manual checklists for the workflows people actually use, and a rule that nothing deploys with failing tests. I look for the cases that break: wrong data, missing permissions, and features that work alone but fail together.

My technical background spans from high-level web frameworks like Laravel down to low-level assembly programming, which gives me a broad understanding of how software systems work at every layer. As a native Amharic speaker with advanced English, I also work on AI data and annotation projects, particularly ones that improve models for underrepresented languages.
